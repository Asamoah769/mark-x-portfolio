Why every shop needs a POS system

A notebook works until the shop gets busy. Here is what a point-of-sale system changes, and what to ask before you buy one.

October 2026, 3 min read

Counting sales in a notebook works until the shop gets busy. A point-of-sale (POS) system records every sale as it happens, so you stop guessing.

What it gives you

  • Faster checkout, with items scanned or tapped instead of added up by hand.
  • Stock that updates by itself, so you know what to reorder.
  • Daily and monthly sales reports without adding up receipts at night.
  • Fewer mistakes, and a clear record of who sold what.

What to ask before you buy

  • Does it keep working when the internet is down?
  • Can it print receipts on the printer you already own?
  • Can you export your own data if you ever switch?
  • Who do you call when something goes wrong?

A good POS helps you spot small losses that are easy to miss in a notebook.

Five signs your business needs a proper logo

If your name looks different on every sign and flyer, customers notice. Here is how to tell it is time, and what a good logo package includes.

October 2026, 3 min read

A logo is more than decoration. It is how people recognise you on a signboard, a flyer or a phone screen. You may need a new one if:

  • Your name is written in a different style on every sign, flyer and post.
  • The logo turns blurry when it is printed large.
  • It only looks right on a white background.
  • Customers cannot tell you apart from the shop next door at a glance.
  • You only have a photo of your logo, not the original file.

What you should receive

  • Full-colour, one-colour and reversed (light on dark) versions.
  • A PNG with a transparent background for posts and screens.
  • A print-ready file for signs, shirts and banners.
  • A short note listing your colours and fonts, so everything stays consistent.

Moving school fees from paper to software

Paper fee registers are slow to search and easy to get wrong. A few habits make the switch to software smooth.

October 2026, 3 min read

Paper registers are hard to search, hard to total, and easy to lose. A fee management system keeps every payment in one place.

What changes

  • Receipts print in seconds with the correct balance.
  • You can see who owes, by class and by term.
  • Term totals appear without recounting.
  • Separate logins mean only the right staff can edit payments.

Tips for a smooth switch

  • Start at the beginning of a term, not in the middle.
  • Enter opening balances carefully, and only once.
  • Run paper and software side by side for a few weeks.
  • Keep backups on a separate drive or cloud account.

Simple computer habits that prevent big problems

Most computer emergencies can be avoided. These habits cost nothing and protect your files.

October 2026, 3 min read

Most computer emergencies are avoidable. These habits cost almost nothing and protect your work.

  • Back up your files. Keep a copy on a separate drive or cloud account, not only on the computer.
  • Install updates. Windows and your apps fix security problems regularly.
  • Use strong, different passwords. A password manager makes this easy.
  • Think before you click. Be careful with email attachments and links from people you do not know.
  • Avoid cracked software. It is a common way for viruses to get in.
  • Replace failing hardware early. A slow, noisy hard drive often fails without warning.

When to ask for help

If files disappear, the computer restarts on its own, or you see pop-ups you did not expect, stop and get help before you lose more.
